Instagram analytics for coaching clients

Convergence Analytics is a private tool run by Volna Group LLC. It copies a client's Instagram post statistics into that client's own coaching dashboard, so the client and their coach can see which videos work and why.

How it works

  1. Your coach sends you a personal connect link.
  2. You approve read-only access to one Instagram account on Meta's own screen.
  3. This page shows a one-time code, which you send to your coach to finish the connection.

What it reads

  • Your public profile name and your posts (caption, date, link, video).
  • The statistics for those posts: views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares and watch time.

What it never does

  • It cannot post, comment, message or change anything on your account.
  • It does not read your messages, and it never sees your password.
  • Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.
