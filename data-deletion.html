Convergence Analytics

Delete my data

You can stop Convergence Analytics from reading your Instagram account, and have everything we hold about it deleted, at any time. There are two steps.

1. Remove the app's access

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and open Settings.
  2. Open Integrations, then Connected apps.
  3. Select Convergence Analytics and choose Remove.

From that moment the app can no longer read your account.

2. Ask us to delete what we already hold

Email [[CONTACT_EMAIL]] with the subject "Delete my Convergence Analytics data" and your Instagram username, or message your coach directly.

What we delete

  • Every row of post data and statistics in your analytics dashboard, including captions, transcripts, on-screen text and thumbnails.
  • Your access token.
  • Your account's entry in the app's settings.

We complete this within [[DELETION_DAYS]] days and reply to confirm when it is done. We do not keep backups of your Instagram data after deletion.
